    Keyboard shortcuts

    Transform
    VMove (select and drag)
    TRotate around the pivot (Shift: 15° steps)
    SScale from the pivot
    AArtboard: drag its handles to resize; the drawing stays centered (Shift: keep proportions)
    Create
    PPen (Enter or Esc to finish)
    R / ERectangle / ellipse (Shift: square or circle)
    GNew empty group
    Shift+GSwitch: wraps the selection as variants (no selection: an empty one)
    Skeleton
    BBone: drag on empty space to create (child of the selected one; from its tip, chained), drag a bone to rotate it
    Ctrl+BBind the selection to the selected bone (no bone: unbind)
    In a poseDragging a bone or its bound artwork rotates the bone in the pose
    Editing
    EnterEdit the points of the selected path
    Click · Ctrl/Shift + clickSelect · add or remove (in Layers, Shift = range)
    Drag on empty spaceMarquee select (Ctrl/Shift: add)
    Ctrl+ASelect all
    Ctrl+GGroup the selection
    Ctrl+DDuplicate
    DelDelete (in point editing: the vertex)
    Arrow keysNudge 1 px (Shift: 10 px)
    Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+ZUndo / redo your changes
    View
    F / 1Fit / 100%
    Wheel · Ctrl+wheelScroll · zoom
    Space + dragPan the view
    Alt+WClose the tab
