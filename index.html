      Keyboard shortcuts
      
      
      
        
      Transform: Select (V) does it all; Move (M), Scale (S) and Rotate (T) do one thing, dragging anywhere
      
        
      Click · Shift+clickPick a part · add it or take it out (also in Layers)
      
        
      Drag a partMove the selection (Shift: straight; Alt: drag a copy)
      
        
      Drag on empty spaceMarquee: picks every part it touches (Shift: adds). A click goes back to the layer
      
        
      Square handlesScale (Shift: keep proportions; Alt: from the center)
      
        
      Round handleRotate (Shift: 15° steps)
      
        
      Arrow keysNudge 1 px (Shift: 10 px)
      
        
      Points: click a picked part again to edit the points of every part of its layer
      
        
      Click · Shift+click · drag on empty spacePick points · add or take out · marquee
      
        
      Click a line or inside a shapePick all its points (Shift: add them); drag to move them. With Add point on, a click on a line adds a point
      
        
      Drag a point · its round knobsMove the picked points · bend the curve (Shift: break a smooth point, or line up a broken one)
      
        
      Alt+click a pointCorner ↔ curve
      
        
      Del · Esc or click on empty spaceDelete the picked points · done
      
        
      Ctrl+D · Ctrl+C · Ctrl+X · Ctrl+VDuplicate · copy · cut · paste into the active layer (pick another layer first to paste there), in the same place
      
        
      Draw
      
        
      PPen: press and drag to place a point (smooth; Sharp corners or Alt: a corner); press on an open end to go on with its line, release on its other end to close it
      
        
      R · ERectangle · ellipse: drag on the canvas (Shift: square · circle). Every shape is a path
      
        
      FCreate Shape: pick the points around a shape (click, box, or a click inside a gap; Shift adds or takes out, also dragging over points); a ring welds two crossing lines
      
        
      Enter / EscCreate Shape: make the shape the picked points close / clear the picks
      
        
      QSelect Shape: shows what a click takes (an element whole, or a shape’s fill or stroke); Alt+click goes down through what is under it; ↑ / ↓ raise or lower it in its layer (Shift: to the top or bottom)
      
        
      HHide Edge: click a stretch of a stroke to hide it (the fill stays), or drag from anywhere across several; click a hidden one, shown dashed, to show it again (a drag that first crosses a hidden one shows them); Alt: the stroke under the topmost one
      
        
      After drawing, a small box asks for the part's name: type it and Enter, or Esc to name it later.
      
        
      New parts go into the selected vector layer (or the layer of the selected part); with none, a new layer is made.
      
        
      View
      
        
      Wheel · Shift+wheelZoom around the pointer (also Ctrl+wheel and a pinch) · move sideways
      
        
      Space+drag · middle button dragMove the view, with any tool
      
        
      Ctrl + · Ctrl −Zoom in · out
      
        
      Ctrl+0 · Ctrl+1 · Shift+2Fit the artboard · 100% · zoom to the selection (or the picked points)
      
        
      Editing
      
        
      Ctrl+Z · Ctrl+Shift+ZUndo · redo your changes
      
        
      DelDelete the selected layer or part
      
        
      F2 or double clickRename the selected layer or part (a part's name is in the options bar)
      
        
      Page Up / Page DownRaise or lower the selected parts or layers one place (Shift: to the top or bottom)
      
        
      Drag in LayersReorder; drop on the middle of a group to put it inside
      
        
      Alt+WClose the tab
