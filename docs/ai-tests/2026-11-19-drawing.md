# AI test: Drawing (part 4) with the `aia` CLI (2026-11-19)

**Setup.** An AI agent with only `node bin/aia.js …` (no repo files, no source) and the images `render` writes.
It learned the tool from `aia help`, `aia help <command>` and `aia guide` (start, points, paths, ops, types).

**Task.** A new document `prueba_ai_face`: a round head, two eyes (each a circle with a smaller pupil circle inside),
and a mouth line across the lower part of the head, welded to the head at both ends. Color it: skin head with the
eyes cut out as holes, separate white eye whites, dark pupils, a red part below the mouth (between mouth and jaw).
Add a hat whose brim line crosses the head outline without being welded, and color the part of the hat that
overlaps the head differently (deal with the crossing). Give the head shape an outline stroke of its own color, put
the pupils above the eye whites, then move the whole head to the right and check that the colors moved with it.

**Result: done, about 60 commands (of them 14 renders), 6 refused (5 of them on purpose to probe errors), 1 silent
bug worked around.** Lines: `head` (8-point ellipse, named), `eye_l/eye_r`, `pupil_l/pupil_r`, `mouth` (open,
`mouth.0` → `head.bl`, `mouth.2` → `head.br`), `hat` (rectangle line). Fills made with `aia fill --at`: `head_fill`
(skin, with two holes along the eyes, found automatically), `jaw_fill` (red), `pupil_*_fill`, `hat_fill` (hat outside
the head) and `hat_overlap` (purple); the eye whites were remade with `aia shape` from the eye points (no hole) so the
pupils could sit above them. `aia fill` on the filled skin gap with `--stroke` gave `head_fill` its own brown stroke.
`aia order … --top` put the pupils over the whites. `aia transform face --move 60,0` moved the layer; every fill went
with it (render checked). The crossing had to be welded by hand (`add-point` + `weld --on`) because
`crossings --weld` put the points in the wrong place.

## What went wrong or was hard

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **`crossings --weld all` (and `--weld 1`) welded at the wrong place and bent the head** | It listed the crossings correctly (`head × hat at 519.96, 210` and `280.04, 210`) but the points it added on the hat were at x 535.9 and 264.16; the head's new vertex shares that hat point, so the head outline got a kink on both sides (visible in the render). The help says "Both lines keep their shape". Worked around with `add-point head --at 519.96,210 --name cr_r` and `weld head.cr_r --on hat` (twice), after which `crossings` said "cross nowhere". This is the main bug of the part. |
| `crossings --weld 3` with no crossings says "--weld is a number from 1 to 0, or all" | Wrong range text; it should say there are no crossings. |
| `aia shape` refusals do not say what is missing | "the picked points do not close a shape: pick every point around it", both for 1 point and for 2 points of different parts. It does not say which stretch is open or which point it could not reach. |
| Auto names of fills are not told up front and cannot be chosen | The red jaw became `head_fill_2`, the purple overlap also `head_fill_2` (after undo). There is no `--id` on `fill`/`shape`; I renamed with an `ops rename`, whose answer reads "created: jaw_fill; gone: head_fill_2" (looks like a delete). |
| `fill --at` is in layer coordinates | After the layer was moved, `fill --at 400,200` hit the hat overlap instead of the skin. Documented, but render shows the artboard, so the AI has to subtract the layer offset itself. |
| `fill … --fill none` on a filled gap leaves an invisible part | It "recolored" `hat_overlap` to no fill instead of removing it; there is no "unfill". I used undo. |
| "the whole head" is ambiguous: part vs. layer | `transform head --move` moved only the head line and its shared points, so the hat's welded brim points moved too and the hat was distorted while eyes stayed. Correct by the rules, but the help could warn that moving a part drags the points welded to it. The layer transform was the right one. |
| Fill's own stroke also strokes its holes and the mouth stretch | The skin's brown stroke shows around the eye holes and along the mouth, not only the head outline. Probably as designed (it is the shape's outline), but "outline stroke" made me expect only the outer edge; nothing in the help says so. |
| `order` with several ids keeps their old relative order | `order pupil_l_fill pupil_l pupil_r_fill pupil_r --top` gave `pupil_l_fill, pupil_r_fill, pupil_l, pupil_r`. Documented ("keep their order among themselves"), fine. |
| No transparent/checkerboard render | Holes cannot be checked on a white background; I had to read `show` to see the `holes` array. `render` has no `--background none`/checker option. |
| Fill message punctuation | "with 2 holes along eye_l; eye_r" (semicolon list) reads oddly. |
| Labels crowd the eyes | `render --labels` labels every auto-named point (`eye_l.eye_l_1`, `hat.hat_1`); small features become unreadable. |

What worked well: `fill --at` found the holes by itself, followed the welded mouth and put the fill under the lines;
`fill` on a filled gap restyles it; welding by name and `add-point --name` are clear; every refusal for `fill` said
the likely cause ("lines that cross must be welded where they meet").

## Parity with the editor (part 4, drawing)

| Editor action | By command? | How / how hard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create Shape: click inside a gap | Yes | `aia fill <doc> <layer> --at x,y`. Easy; holes are found automatically. Coordinates are the layer's. |
| Create Shape: pick points (click, Shift to add/unpick) | Yes | `aia shape <doc> <layer> <point>...`. Medium: you must list every point around it, and the error does not say what is missing. No "unpick" (just leave it out). |
| Create Shape: box selection of points | Partly | No area selection by command; `layer.group` is accepted as a list of points, so a point group can stand in for a box. Box-by-rect (`--in x,y,w,h`) does not exist. |
| Checkerboard behind the canvas (see holes) | No | `render` always draws the background; holes are only seen in `show`. |
| Weld a crossing with a click on its ring | Yes, but buggy | `aia crossings <doc> <layer>` lists them numbered, `--weld n/all` welds; it welded at the wrong place in this test. Manual workaround: `add-point` + `weld --on`. |
| Inspector: element's own fill/stroke | Yes | `aia fill … --at` on a filled gap with `--fill/--stroke/--stroke-width`, or `ops set` on the part. There is no `aia style <part>` that works by id. |
| Inspector: shapes list as `<line>_fill` / `<line>_stroke` pairs | Partly | The CLI made one part (`head_fill`) with both fill and stroke; no `<line>_stroke` part appeared, and nothing in help/guide mentions the pair naming. Either the CLI and the editor make different structures, or the guide does not explain it. |
| Inspector: shape order (raise, lower, delete) | Yes | `aia order <id> --up/--down/--top/--bottom`, `ops delete`. Easy. |
| Inspector: Flip | Yes | `aia transform <id> --scale -1,1`. |
| Shape dropdown (rename) | Yes | `ops rename`. No `--id` at creation. |

## Higher-level commands I would have wanted (most useful first)

1. A working `crossings --weld` (the bug above); then `--weld` that prints where it put each point.
2. `--id <name>` on `fill` and `shape` (and on `rect`/`ellipse` it already is the id), so fills get meaningful names.
3. `aia style <doc> <part> [--fill …] [--stroke …]` to restyle a part by id, and `aia unfill <doc> <layer> --at x,y`.
4. `aia render --checker` (or `--background none` to a transparent PNG) to see holes.
5. `shape` errors that name the open stretch or the unreachable point; `shape --box x,y,w,h` to pick points in an area.
6. `aia shapes <doc> <line>`: list the fills that follow a line, with their order (the Inspector's shape list).
7. A note in `help transform`: moving a part also moves points welded to it in other parts.
